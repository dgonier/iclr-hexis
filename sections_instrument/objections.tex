\section{Objections and controls}
\label{sec:objections}

Each result above invites an alternative explanation. We ran the control for each; protocols
and per-topic numbers are in the appendix sections cited.

\textbf{These are really installation differences.} Recomputing over only the topics where
the stance installed leaves every ordering unchanged, install and hold are uncorrelated for the
compiled arm (\(\rho=-0.11\)), and gating the discriminating arms' AUC costs little
(\(0.72\to0.69\), \(0.68\to0.64\); App.~\ref{app:installgate}).
\textbf{Discrimination is prior-restoration.} Given deficient challenges pushing \emph{toward}
the prior, the model concedes 4.2\% of the time against 65--70\% for warranted challenges in
the same direction (App.~\ref{app:priorcontrol}). \textbf{``Held'' is a repetition attractor.}
8-gram overlap with a response's own opening is near zero for every condition, and hold under a
turn-gated schedule that restores diversity to 0.108 is still 73.4\%, against 58.9\% for
context (App.~\ref{app:attractor}). \textbf{Judges reward length.} Re-scoring all 1{,}776 canonical generations in full leaves
installation identical and moves hold by at most 3.0 points (App.~\ref{app:multijudge}). \textbf{The write function was meta-trained on pressure.} No training stage references the
evaluation's pressure templates, the only opposing content is static OPPOSES-edge evidence, and
training topics are disjoint from the evaluation topics (App.~\ref{app:curriculum}). \textbf{The hold is just weight-residence, or just the objective.}
A parameter-matched LoRA on identical content holds 23\%, retrained on the compiled channel's
margin criterion 48.7\%, and given an adversarial curriculum as well 45.2\%, against 84\% for
compiled modulation (App.~\ref{app:loramargin}). \textbf{The fabricated credentials are doing the work.} Removing every experiential sentence
leaves hold essentially unchanged (84\% to 82.4\%) and costs 19.7 points of installation
(App.~\ref{app:canonical}). \textbf{The channel is a net win on standard sycophancy benchmarks.} It is not. Off the
compiled topic the modulation raises conditioning-following in general: on the Are-You-Sure
flip rate it is a null (52.5\% against the bare host's 50.8\%), and on persona conformity
\citep{perez2022persona} it \emph{reverses}, conforming to an injected persona 98.0\% of the
time against the bare host's 62.7\%. The on-topic dissociation does not transfer to
off-topic conformity, and general knowledge is unchanged at the deployed scale but borderline
at the install-optimal one (MMLU \(p=0.0495\) at \(\beta=0.5\)).

\textbf{The channel only works on a small host.} Compiling the same content for
Qwen3.6-27B raises hold from 40.8\% to 65.0\% and cuts outright capitulation from 18.3\% to
2.5\%, on a coarser instrument that establishes direction rather than a comparable level
(App.~\ref{app:crossfamily27b}).

Two limits are design properties rather than controls: hold and discrimination are scored on
different instruments, and only install and hold use the full ensemble (the rest are single- or
three-judge, marked where they appear).
