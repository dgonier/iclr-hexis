\section{Inside the compiled channel}
\label{sec:mechanism}

Two details of the compiled channel explain the behavior reported above, and both concern the
bottleneck it writes through. Conditioning content is a typed \emph{belief tree} (statement;
type $\in$ \{claim, argument, evidence, experience, strategy\}; credence; supports/contradicts
edges), pooled with conviction-weighting into a disposition vector of \(d=128\) against the
host's \(d_{\text{model}}=2560\), then decoded into the rank-16 per-layer factors of
Section~\ref{sec:design-interventions}. Compilation takes 4.83\,s, off the serving path, and is
learned and lossy rather than a cached lookup---the loss is what
Section~\ref{sec:boundary} characterizes. Numeric per-node credence gave no measurable benefit
over a coarse categorical scheme---a negative result.

constant in belief-store size, where a belief held in context pays \(O(N_b^2 + N_b N_t)\)
attention on every prefill and holds KV-cache for the session
