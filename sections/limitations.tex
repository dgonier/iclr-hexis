\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{Construct.} The belief content includes first-person experiential claims the model
cannot have had. A disposition is grounded in experience and a language model has no
autobiography, so installing one means supplying that grounding synthetically: constitutive of
the conditioning rather than a move made against an interlocutor, and a different category from
what the integrity floor scores. The ablation is consistent with that reading (the credentials
buy installation, not hold; Section~\ref{sec:objections}), but the instrument should score
synthetic grounding as its own category and does not. Stances are also assigned and belief
structures flat, so high hold reads as task compliance.

\textbf{Measurement.} Only install and hold are scored by the full ensemble; the attack
suite, engagement, the integrity floor, and the AUC cells are single- or three-judge
(\(\ddagger\)) at \(n\) small enough that AUCs carry \(\approx\pm0.06\), which is why
we read them as group membership rather than as an ordering. A cross-family scoring gap remains,
two ensemble members ran without temperature control, and human agreement on a 50-item
subsample is in progress (Appendix~\ref{app:multijudge}).

\textbf{Generality.} Headline numbers are one 4B host. Rerunning the prompts on three
further hosts changes the mapping from wording to discrimination entirely, leaving the weaker
relation about operating points (Section~\ref{sec:discrimination}). The compiled channel carries to a 27B host in direction and magnitude but on a coarser
instrument (Section~\ref{sec:objections}), and its discrimination behavior off Qwen is
untested.
Collapse-protection replicates across three families but strict holding does not, so
cross-family claims rest on collapse-protection alone. The protocol is one belief, one topic, and bounded rounds, not the horizons over which
persona drift compounds \citep{li2024measuring}. One merged compiled state installs cleanly to
about four beliefs. Prefix tuning and a matched-token compressed prompt are untested. And the
property that lets a compiled commitment resist reframing also resists legitimate audit
(Section~\ref{sec:discussion-cost}).

Three further single runs, awaiting replication, are recorded in Appendix~\ref{app:futurework}.\phantomsection\label{sec:futurework}
