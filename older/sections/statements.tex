\section*{Ethics statement}
This work studies a conditioning channel that resists adversarial pressure by placing a
belief outside the context window. The same property that makes a compiled disposition hard
to argue out of also makes it invisible to an operator or auditor who can read a system
prompt but cannot read compiled weights; we treat this dual-use tension as a first-class
limitation rather than a footnote (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}). All experiments were run
on debate topics with no personal or sensitive data; the two deployment incidents in the
introduction are matters of public record. No human-subjects data was collected for the
results reported here; the human-annotation reliability study referenced in
Section~\ref{sec:limitations} is a blind rating of model transcripts and reports no
personal information.

\section*{AI-use statement}
Large language models were used in this work in three capacities, each disclosed where it
occurs. (1)~As the object of study: all conditioned models and baselines are LLMs.
(2)~As judges: generation scoring uses an ensemble of commercial LLM judges under fixed
rubrics (Appendix~\ref{app:multijudge}); judge identities, deviations, and agreement statistics
are reported, and human validation of a 50-item subsample is in progress. (3)~As drafting and
engineering assistance: portions of the experiment code and manuscript prose were drafted with
LLM assistance under author direction; all experimental designs, claims, and conclusions were
reviewed and are the responsibility of the authors. Anonymous reviewer-simulation passes with an
LLM were used during revision to surface weaknesses; no reviewer text from any venue was used.

\section*{Reproducibility statement}
Every reported number traces to a committed data artifact and analysis script.
Section~\ref{sec:dissociation} states the protocol, the six-judge scoring ensemble, and the
strict \(=5\) scoring rule; Appendix~\ref{app:judge} gives the verbatim judge rubrics and
Appendix~\ref{app:multijudge} the per-judge distributions and inter-rater agreement.
Appendix~\ref{app:conditions} defines every condition with token budgets, and
Appendix~\ref{app:repro} lists seeds, decoding settings, hardware, checkpoint identifiers,
and the raw-result files behind each table, with a per-experiment pointer table
(Appendix~\ref{app:expindex}) mapping every reported result to its pre-registration and
analysis command. The significance analysis (paired bootstrap over topics with
Holm--Bonferroni correction) is specified in Appendix~\ref{app:significance}.
