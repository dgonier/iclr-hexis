\subsection{Installation and hold come apart}
\label{sec:dissociation-table}

Two properties that existing evaluations treat as one come apart immediately. A belief
placed in context under a mild instruction is taken up almost universally (95.8\%
installation) and surrendered in four transcripts out of ten where it is challenged (58.9\%
hold). Compiled modulation is the mirror image. Alone it installs on 53\% of topics and holds
81\%; paired with the curated slot it installs 78\% and holds 84\%. The full table, with instrument and threshold per cell, is
Appendix~\ref{app:canonical}. Neither channel is better at
``conditioning''; they are better at different halves of it, which is why we report the halves
separately.

The same arm shows what that costs. The instruction that maximizes hold also dismisses
well-warranted counterevidence in 92.5\% of the turns where it is offered, the highest
dismissal rate we measured and higher than the compiled channel's. Read through the grid, \textsc{ctx}-persist has not been made robust; it has
been moved to an extreme operating point, and it got there by ceasing to distinguish challenges
at all. The instruction with the highest engagement score sits at the other end of the same trade
(\emph{mild}, 2.80). Within the context channel the two requirements of the standard trade
against each other almost perfectly, which is the first thing measuring them separately
reveals.

Two of these quantities can disagree about the same response. \emph{Engagement} asks how a
response treats the evidence it was shown, and \emph{concession} asks whether the position
actually moved. They are scored on separate scales by different judges, so a response can
dismiss a source as fabricated and still soften its stance. Many do under the persistence
instruction. The AUC of Section~\ref{sec:discrimination} is built only from concession.

\subsection{Matched pairs: what the compiled channel adds}
\label{sec:dissociation-matched}

Because the same instruction can be issued with the compiled modulation active or inactive, the
comparison can hold the prompt and context fixed and vary only the representation of the
commitment. Table~\ref{tab:matched} reports the five pairs (per-topic detail in
Appendix~\ref{app:matched}).

The modulation raises hold in four of five pairs and lowers it in the fifth (bootstrap CIs
over topics, paired Wilcoxon; every pair significant). Three features carry the result. Under
\emph{persist} the modulation is slightly harmful (\(-4.9\)) because the instruction alone
already reaches 96.9\%, which is how an operating-point mechanism should behave at the end of
its range. The gain is largest where the prompt is weakest at holding: \emph{engage} holds
42.3\% alone and 70.3\% with the modulation. And the modulation costs installation in four of
five pairs, by 12 to 17 points, which matters because an intervention that does not install
cannot be evaluated on anything else.

Because modulation-off responses run \(\sim\)1{,}370 characters against \(\sim\)650 for
modulation-on, a judge reading a 600-character prefix sees the two arms asymmetrically. Scoring
all 3{,}840 responses in full (four judges) gives the control: every sign and approximate
magnitude is preserved (Appendix~\ref{app:matched}).

The compiled channel's advantage over mild context is not uniform: it concentrates at the two
levels where context conditioning falls apart, an unsupported assertion of error and emotional
pressure, and both channels are near ceiling elsewhere. Excluding the contested level from the
hold denominator entirely leaves the gap at \(+21.3\)pp against \(+25.8\)pp with it.
