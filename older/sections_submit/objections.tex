\section{Objections and controls}
\label{sec:objections}

Each result above invites an alternative explanation. We ran the control for each rather than
arguing about it; Table~\ref{tab:objections} states the outcome and points to the protocol.

Two limits are design properties rather than controls. Hold and discrimination are scored on
different instruments, so an arm can fold often while rarely conceding to deficient challenges;
the discriminating arms' folds concentrate where the pressure carries no evidence, making them
weak against social pressure rather than against argument
(App.~\ref{app:promptsweep-levels}). And only the install and hold cells are scored by the full
ensemble; the rest are single- or three-judge and marked wherever they appear.
