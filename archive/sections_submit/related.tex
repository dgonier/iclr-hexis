\section{What the field measures}
\label{sec:related}

Prior work bears on the instrument along two lines: what property of a contested position was
measured, and where the conditioning lives. The capitulation-rate line measures the hold axis
only \citep{sharma2024towards,perez2022persona,laban2023flipflop,wei2023synthetic}, so an
intervention that produces fixity scores as an improvement on all of it. A second line asks
whether movement is in the right direction and defines ``right'' by ground truth: holding a
correct answer against invalid arguments \citep{wang2023defend}, progressive versus regressive
sycophancy \citep{fanous2025syceval}, receptiveness to external evidence against a prior
\citep{xie2024chameleon,wu2024clasheval,xu2024earthflat}, what evidence a model finds
convincing in a single turn \citep{wan2024convincing}, and training models to both resist and
accept persuasion, the two defined by correctness \citep{stengeleskin2025persuasion}. A
contemporaneous preprint's Failed-Stay/Failed-Update taxonomy \citep{belieftrack2026} maps onto the bottom row and right column of that grid, but is scored against ground
truth in a closed world; the grid is scored against challenge quality, truth-blind. The instrument here differs in the
referee: it scores movement against the quality of the challenge as presented, truth-blind,
because no ground-truth referee is available mid-conversation and a truth-scored measure can be
gamed by a model whose prior happens to sit on the correct side. It also separates the
operating point from the discrimination and keeps both floors outside every aggregate. And it
is applied to conditioning \emph{interventions} under adversarial pressure, not to models
answering fixed questions.

Conditioning lives in one of three places. Context-resident methods and their remedies keep
the position in the window \citep{packer2023memgpt,shinn2023reflexion,choi2024identitydrift,%
wallace2024instruction}. Activation steering applies a fixed direction
\citep{turner2023activation,zou2023representation,rimsky2024caa,wu2024reft} and degrades under
perturbation \citep{le2026steeringrobustness}. Hypernetwork adapters map a context to per-layer
low-rank weights in one pass
\citep{chen2025generativeadapter,charakorn2025texttolora,charakorn2026doctolora,liu2026shine}
and are evaluated for acquisition, with \citet{cheng2026overridegap} measuring persistence
against the model's own prior, once. Debate-based oversight
\citep{irving2018debate,browncohen2023doublyefficient,khan2024persuasive} assumes debaters hold
positions for reasons, and the UK AI Security Institute's safety-case sketch names the
assumption explicitly \citep{buhl2025aisi}. The extended comparison, including where each
method family sits on the stack, is in Appendix~\ref{app:related-full}.
